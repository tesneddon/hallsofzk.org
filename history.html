Books & Articles

The Halls of ZK is a game about Digital, written by people who worked there. These books, published by Digital itself, tell the story of the company, its engineering and VMS, and are a good way to get to know the world the game pokes fun at.

  • Digital Equipment Corporation: Nineteen Fifty-Seven to the Present (1978, PDF, 60 MB)
  • Engineering Orientation Manual (EL-ENGRS-OM-000, April 1982, PDF, 10 MB)
  • Digital at Work: Snapshots from the First Thirty-Five Years (1992, PDF, 25 MB)
  • OpenVMS 1977–1997… and Beyond (1997, PDF, 2.5 MB)
