Map

Every adventurer needs a map! Although, hopefully you don;t spend too much time studying it, where's the fun in that? There are three floors, joined by stairwells and a lift.

Where an exit doesn't lead to the square next door, a note in the room says where it goes: SE ▸ 31 means south east takes you to room 31. In the twisty cubicles, that is most of them! Things found lying about at the start are written in green.

Blue Level

The upper floor and the way in, rooms 1 to 40.

Yellow Level

The middle floor, rooms 41 to 75.

Red Level

The lower floor, rooms 76 to 90.
