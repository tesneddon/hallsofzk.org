Barcode

One of the most well known features of the former ZKO facility is the barcode on the wall at the entrance of ZKO1. There have been a number of posts on the usenet group comp.os.vms like this one and this other one where the translation has been a hot topic.

When ZK originally opened it started out as a 26 character, 7-bit ASCII string that read: 

digitalsoftwa
reengineering

Eventually, some time in the early 1990s the wall was altered. It was re-painted from orange and red to black and grey. The message was also changed, purportedly due to management re-shuffles and culture changes within DEC. The message became: 

Customers Win
WhenWeDeliver

Over time, some of the bars rusted and were replaced. Unfortunately the replacements were not always matched in colour. Maybe due to a lack of appropriate spares. Possibly even a lack of knowledge or care by those who did the work. As a result, the final barcoded incantation became: 

Customers Win
?henWeFeliver

In 2007 HP sold the ZKO facility to a local property developer, John J. Flatley Co. and for a time the barcode remained.

Nashua Tech Park

Following the purchase of the ZKO facility, the different ZK offices were renamed, ZKO1 became 200 Innovation Way, ZKO2 became 300 Innovation Way and ZKO3 became 100 Innovation Way.

Part of the redevelopment of the facility into the Nashua Technology Park, a part of the larger Gateway Hills property development, was to remove the barcode and replace it with a covered walkway.

However, although removed and replaced with a walkway, the barcode continues to live on in another form. The bars have been used to construct a new barcode on the cafeteria wall in 200 Innovation Way.

The bars are now accompanied by a plaque explaining the history of the ASCII character set as well as details of how to read the barcode. The new barcode is arranged in a series of 5-bit bytes that now reads: 

NASHUATECHPARK
